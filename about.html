Meet the person behind the work

Samuel Alema-Mensah, FCCA, CISA

Co-Founder & Director, Tehillah Digital Business Solutions Limited

Well-proven frameworks, applied to your real business and explained in plain English that your board and your bank manager can both follow.

I've spent twenty years in IT risk, controls, internal audit and governance. I started at PwC in Ghana and London, delivering IT general controls, SOX and ERP security reviews for FTSE and financial services clients. I then spent almost eleven years at a multinational listed retail and healthcare group, in progressively senior roles across IT controls, global financial control and internal audit. There I led the continuous controls monitoring programme for non-US locations, ran IT SOX and ICFR programmes with a team of up to twelve, and gave assurance over financially significant systems, working closely with CIOs and external auditors.

I co-founded Tehillah Digital to bring that same care to growing businesses, start-ups and scale-ups that don't yet have an internal audit team. I'm currently a mentee on The Cyber Scheme's Cyber Talent Incubator Network and am preparing for the VA+ (Vulnerability Assessment Plus) assessment, extending my audit background into cyber risk and hands-on vulnerability assessment.
