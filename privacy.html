Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Tehillah Digital Business Solutions Limited ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website and when we get in touch with businesses, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

We are a company registered in England & Wales (company number 17417070) with our registered office in Manchester, England, M40 8WN. We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. You can reach us at hello@tehillahdigital.co.uk or on 07592 297 682.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give one) and whatever you tell us in your message. If you email or call us, we keep a record of that conversation. We also keep the business contact details of people at organisations we think could benefit from our services, such as a company's published general email address or phone number and the name of the organisation.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

How and why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to agree and deliver the work. We do this because it is necessary to respond to you and to prepare or perform a contract with you.
  • To contact businesses about our services. We may email or phone organisations, using business contact details, to introduce what we do. We do this under our legitimate interest in developing our business. We identify ourselves, keep the contact short and relevant, and always include a way to opt out. We do not cold-email individuals or sole traders without their consent, and we check numbers against the Telephone Preference Service before we call.
  • To keep our records and meet our legal duties, such as accounting and tax requirements.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host, email and document providers, and professional advisers. They may only use it to provide their service to us. If we ever need to transfer information outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and business contact records for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as we need them to deliver the work and to meet legal and professional obligations, usually six years after an engagement ends. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep only your name and email address on a suppression list so that we do not contact you again.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our use of it, ask us to restrict it, and ask for it to be passed to you or another provider. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying "stop" to any email or telling us on a call. To use any of these rights, email hello@tehillahdigital.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to hello@tehillahdigital.co.uk.
